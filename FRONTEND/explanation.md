A PCB Manufacturing ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) system is a centralized software platform designed to manage end-to-end printed circuit board manufacturing operations, from customer enquiry and design files to production, quality inspection, inventory control, dispatch, and financial tracking.

Unlike generic ERPs, a PCB ERP is process-aware of electronics manufacturing constraints such as:

Multilayer stackups

Panelization and routing

Process-stage WIP tracking

Lot and serial traceability

Quality checkpoints (AOI, E-test)

Material chemistry, copper weights, TG values

Tight delivery timelines and rework cycles

A PCB ERP becomes the single source of truth across sales, engineering, production, quality, stores, procurement, and management.

2. Core Objectives of a PCB ERP

A well-designed PCB manufacturing ERP aims to:

Ensure on-time delivery

Maintain full traceability (order → panel → lot → process → inspection)

Reduce scrap, rework, and downtime

Optimize inventory and procurement

Provide real-time visibility of WIP

Standardize workflows across departments

Support compliance and audits

Enable data-driven decisions

4. Major Modules of a PCB Manufacturing ERP
4.1 Sales & Customer Management
Purpose

Handles customer enquiries, quotations, orders, and communication.

Key Capabilities

Customer master data

RFQ capture (board specs, layers, quantity, delivery)

Quotation with costing logic

Quote revisions & approvals

Sales order creation

Order status visibility

Customer history & repeat jobs

Why it matters

Sales feeds accurate demand into planning and ensures engineering feasibility before commitments.

4.2 Engineering (DFM / CAM)
Purpose

Ensures manufacturability before production begins.

Key Capabilities

Gerber & drill file upload

DFM checks:

Track/space

Annular ring

Hole aspect ratio

Stackup validation

Panelization rules

Tooling data

CAM outputs

Engineering change tracking

Why it matters

Most PCB failures originate from poor DFM. This module prevents costly rework and scrap.

4.3 Bill of Materials (BOM) & Routing
Purpose

Defines what is needed and how it will be made.

Key Capabilities

BOM per PCB type (FR4, copper foil, chemicals, soldermask)

Multilevel BOMs (for multilayer boards)

Process routing:

Imaging

Etching

Lamination

Drilling

Plating

AOI

E-Test

Standard vs custom routes

Revision control

Why it matters

Routing is the backbone of WIP tracking, scheduling, and costing.

4.4 Production Planning & Work Orders
Purpose

Converts orders into executable manufacturing tasks.

Key Capabilities

Work order creation

Batch / panel planning

Due date planning

Capacity constraints

Machine and line assignment

Work order status lifecycle:

Planned

Released

In-Progress

Hold

Completed

Why it matters

This module synchronizes demand, resources, and time.

4.5 Shopfloor Execution (WIP Tracking)
Purpose

Tracks boards as they move through manufacturing stages.

Key Capabilities

Stage-wise WIP updates

Operator inputs

Start/stop timestamps

Yield capture

Rework flags

Lot splitting / merging

Real-time WIP dashboards

Why it matters

Gives live visibility into production health and bottlenecks.

4.6 Quality Management (QA/QC)
Purpose

Ensures compliance, reliability, and customer satisfaction.

Key Capabilities

Incoming inspection

In-process inspection

AOI results

E-test pass/fail

Final inspection

NCR (Non-Conformance Reports)

CAPA (Corrective & Preventive Actions)

Certificate of Compliance (CoC)

Why it matters

PCB quality failures are expensive and reputation-damaging. ERP enforces quality gates.

4.7 Inventory & Stores Management
Purpose

Controls material availability and traceability.

Key Capabilities

Raw material inventory

WIP inventory

Finished goods

Lot and batch tracking

Stock ledger

Reorder levels

Material issue & receipt

Scrap and adjustments

Why it matters

Material shortages or excess stock directly impact delivery and cost.

4.8 Procurement & Supplier Management
Purpose

Ensures timely availability of materials.

Key Capabilities

Supplier master

Purchase requisitions

Purchase orders

GRN (Goods Receipt Note)

Supplier lead times

Price history

Supplier performance metrics

Why it matters

PCB manufacturing depends on chemicals, laminates, copper foil—any delay stalls production.

4.9 Dispatch & Logistics
Purpose

Manages packing and shipment.

Key Capabilities

Packing list generation

Labeling

Courier integration

Shipment tracking

Delivery confirmation

Why it matters

Late or wrong dispatch negates all upstream efficiency.

4.10 Maintenance Management
Purpose

Maintains machine uptime.

Key Capabilities

Equipment master

Preventive maintenance schedules

Breakdown logging

Spare parts tracking

Downtime analysis

Why it matters

Unplanned machine failure is a production killer.

4.11 Finance & Costing (Optional / Integrated)
Purpose

Tracks revenue, cost, and profitability.

Key Capabilities

Order costing

WIP valuation

Inventory valuation

Invoicing

Tax/GST

Profitability reports

4.12 Reporting & Analytics
Purpose

Provides decision-making insights.

Examples

On-time delivery %

Yield & scrap trends

WIP aging

Machine utilization (OEE)

Inventory turnover

Supplier performance
